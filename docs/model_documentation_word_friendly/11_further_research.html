Section 11: Further Research

Based on our experience developing and testing the IRIS system, we have identified several promising avenues for further research and enhancement. These areas represent tactical and strategic priorities for improving the model, with associated timelines for testing and implementation.

Identified Limitations and Research Priorities

Research Area Limitation Identified Proposed Solution Timeline Impact Assessment
Response Latency Complex queries with multiple database searches can take >30 seconds to respond Implement parallel database querying and optimize token usage in prompts Q3 2025 Medium - Will improve user experience but not affect accuracy
Visual Content Handling Limited ability to process and present complex financial diagrams and flowcharts Enhance vision model integration and implement structured representation of visual elements Q4 2025 Medium-High - Improves comprehensiveness for visually-oriented content
Source Conflict Resolution When sources contain conflicting guidance, resolution logic could be improved Develop enhanced source prioritization framework with clearer authority hierarchies Q2 2025 High - Critical for ensuring correct information precedence
Multi-language Support System currently supports only English queries and content Evaluate approaches for supporting additional languages in both queries and responses 2026 Low - Currently not a business requirement but may be in future

Implementation Plans

Near-Term Priorities (Next 6 Months)

The most immediate research priority is improving source conflict resolution. Our tactical plan includes:

  1. Analyzing the test cases where conflicting information was present to identify patterns (Q1 2025)
  2. Developing an enhanced source authority framework with clear hierarchies for different document types (Q1 2025)
  3. Implementing and testing improved prompting techniques for the Summarizer agent to better handle conflicting information (Q2 2025)
  4. Conducting targeted testing with scenarios specifically designed to include conflicting guidance (Q2 2025)

Expected outcome: Improved ability to identify and resolve conflicts between different information sources, with explicit indication of which source takes precedence and why.

Medium-Term Priorities (6-12 Months)

Response latency improvement represents our second-highest priority. Our approach will include:

  1. Implementing parallel database querying to reduce sequential processing time (Q3 2025)
  2. Optimizing prompt structures to reduce token usage while maintaining effectiveness (Q3 2025)
  3. Exploring two-pass approach where preliminary results are shown while more comprehensive research continues (Q4 2025)

Visual content handling will be addressed in parallel, with a focus on:

  1. Enhancing the vision model integration to better process complex financial diagrams (Q3 2025)
  2. Developing structured representation methods for visual information in text-based responses (Q4 2025)
  3. Testing with visually rich documents like cheat sheets and technical publications (Q4 2025)

Long-Term Research (Beyond 12 Months)

Multi-language support remains a longer-term research direction, contingent on business requirements evolving to necessitate this capability. Initial explorations will focus on evaluating the feasibility and potential approaches rather than immediate implementation.

Materiality and Impact Assessment

Among the identified research areas, source conflict resolution has the highest potential impact on model performance. When different knowledge sources contain conflicting guidance (e.g., between IASB standards and internal policies), ensuring the system correctly prioritizes the more authoritative source is critical for maintaining response accuracy. Our testing indicates this issue affects approximately 5% of queries but could have significant implications when it occurs.

Response latency improvements will enhance user experience but do not directly impact response accuracy. However, reduced latency may increase user adoption and satisfaction, indirectly improving the model's business value.

Visual content handling improvements will have a moderate to high impact on certain document types, particularly cheat sheets and process flows, which rely heavily on visual elements to convey complex information.

All research directions will be pursued with a focus on maintaining or enhancing the current high levels of accuracy demonstrated in our testing, while improving user experience and expanding the model's capabilities.
