Implementation considerations across platforms

When selecting a platform for Pillar Two compliance, organizations should consider:

  1. Existing systems integration: Organizations already using one of these platforms for tax reporting will find a natural extension path, though OneStream users will require partner solutions regardless.
  2. Implementation timelines: Oracle and Longview appear to offer faster implementation paths, while SAP typically requires 6-12 months for comprehensive implementation.
  3. Out-of-box vs. customization balance: Oracle and Longview provide the most comprehensive native functionality, Thomson Reuters offers a hybrid approach through Orbitax integration, while OneStream and SAP require significant customization.
  4. Data management capabilities: All platforms offer data integration tools, but Thomson Reuters stands out with 90+ pre-built connections while SAP requires the most extensive custom integration work.
  5. Future-proofing: All vendors commit to ongoing updates, but implementation approaches with less customization (Oracle, Longview) may be easier to maintain as regulations evolve.

Conclusion: Selecting the optimal Pillar Two platform

For organizations prioritizing out-of-box functionality with minimal customization, Oracle EPM Tax Reporting offers the most comprehensive native solution, though its phased release means some capabilities are still maturing. Longview Tax provides similar native capabilities with particularly strong Excel integration and real-time calculation advantages.

Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE offers a robust solution through its Orbitax partnership, with extensive jurisdictional coverage, though the dual-platform approach increases implementation complexity.

OneStream users face the greatest customization requirements, being entirely dependent on partner solutions, while SAP offers powerful calculation capabilities but typically requires the most extensive implementation effort and customization.

The optimal choice depends heavily on an organization's existing technology landscape, with the most efficient path typically being to extend current tax reporting platforms rather than implementing entirely new solutions solely for Pillar Two compliance.

Final Recommendation: The Top Choice

Based on the comprehensive analysis, a scoring approach considering native functionality, implementation ease, customization needs, data management, and future-proofing points to a preferred solution for organizations seeking the most balanced offering.

Key Scoring Considerations:

  • Native/Out-of-the-Box Functionality: Platforms with comprehensive, built-in Pillar Two features score highest.
  • Implementation Speed & Complexity: Faster, less complex implementations are preferred.
  • Customization Required: Solutions requiring minimal customization are advantageous.
  • Data Management & Integration: Robust, integrated data capabilities are crucial.
  • Future-Proofing: Ease of maintenance and adaptation to evolving regulations is key.

While several platforms offer strong capabilities, Oracle EPM Tax Reporting and Longview Tax consistently emerge as top contenders for organizations prioritizing a swift, comprehensive, and relatively less customization-heavy implementation.

Final Choice: Oracle EPM Tax Reporting

Reasoning:

  1. Most Comprehensive Native Solution: Oracle EPM offers a purpose-built module with complete native implementation of IIR, UTPR, and QDMTT methodologies. This significantly reduces reliance on third-party integrations or extensive custom builds compared to SAP or OneStream.
  2. Balanced Implementation Effort: While noted as "Moderate to High" complexity, the availability of partner accelerators can significantly shorten implementation timelines (e.g., Deloitte's reported six-week implementations). This offers a more predictable path than the potentially very long and resource-intensive SAP implementations or the partner-dependent OneStream approach.
  3. Strong Data Management and Integration: Its integrated data management features, automated collection from diverse sources, and transformation capabilities are critical for Pillar Two's data-intensive nature. This is a step above solutions that might require more manual or complex data integration setups.
  4. Future-Proofing and Vendor Commitment: As a major enterprise software provider, Oracle's commitment to ongoing development and updates for its EPM suite, including the Pillar Two module, provides a degree of confidence in adapting to future regulatory changes. Its phased rollout, while a current limitation for some features, also indicates active development.
  5. Edge over Longview: While Longview Tax is a very strong competitor with similar native strengths and excellent Excel integration, Oracle EPM's broader suite integration capabilities and potentially larger ecosystem for support and accelerators give it a slight edge for organizations looking for an enterprise-grade, comprehensive solution.

Therefore, for an organization seeking the optimal balance of comprehensive out-of-the-box Pillar Two functionality, manageable implementation timelines, and robust data handling, Oracle EPM Tax Reporting is the recommended platform. However, Longview Tax remains an excellent alternative, particularly for companies with a strong preference for Excel integration or seeking a standalone Pillar Two module.
