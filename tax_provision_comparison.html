Comparison of Oracle EPM Tax Reporting vs. Leading Tax Provision Solutions

Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive comparison of Oracle Enterprise Performance Management (EPM) Tax Reporting (Oracle TRCS) versus other leading tax provision solutions, including Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE Tax Provision, Insightsoftware Longview Tax, Vertex Enterprise, and CSC Corptax. The analysis covers functional capabilities, technical architecture, implementation support, user experience, cost considerations, situational suitability, and use-case scenarios to help inform decision-making.

Introduction

Tax provision software is critical for corporate tax departments to calculate and report income tax accruals accurately and efficiently. This report compares Oracle EPM Tax Reporting Cloud Service (TRCS) with leading competitors across key evaluation areas. A weighted scoring matrix and scenario-based decision framework are included to aid in objective decision-making.

1. Functional Capabilities

Leading tax provision systems offer a range of core features to streamline the tax provision process, ensure compliance with accounting standards (ASC 740 / IAS 12), and facilitate reporting.

Core Tax Provision and Compliance Features

All major solutions support automation of the year-end and interim tax provision process, including calculations of current and deferred tax, effective tax rate (ETR) reporting, and tax journal entry generation:

  • Oracle Tax Reporting Cloud can automatically book temporary and permanent differences based on predefined rules, reducing manual effort.
  • Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE automates calculating corporate tax estimates "in seconds" and provides one platform for current and deferred tax calculations.
  • Longview Tax offers pre-configured tax calculations and reporting templates to compute global tax charge and deferred taxes.
  • Vertex Enterprise supports the full cycle of tax processes by transforming financial data into tax-ready formats for each jurisdiction.
  • CSC Corptax automates data collection and tax computations in a centralized database.

Regulatory Reporting (Tax Footnotes and Disclosures)

Each solution facilitates preparation of tax footnotes for financial statements and regulatory filings:

  • Oracle TRCS includes out-of-the-box reports for tax provision and deferred tax rollforwards, and supports both U.S. GAAP and IFRS reporting requirements.
  • Longview Tax has added Tax Transparency modules (CbCR) and is evolving to handle BEPS 2.0 Pillar Two global minimum tax reporting in a single solution.
  • ONESOURCE integrates with an Uncertain Tax Positions module and offers pre-built dashboards for visualizing provision results.
  • Vertex focuses on consolidating data by currency and accounting standard for accurate jurisdictional reporting.
  • Corptax provides standard tax reports and is continuously updated for changes in tax regulations.

Data Management and Integration

Data integration capabilities distinguish these solutions:

  • Oracle TRCS integrates seamlessly with Oracle's financial systems and provides a central data store for tax adjustments.
  • ONESOURCE offers data import utilities and ready-made connectors, including a certified SAP S/4HANA connector.
  • Longview Tax emphasizes Excel integration and interfaces with other Longview solutions to create a unified financial and tax data environment.
  • Vertex Enterprise functions as a tax data warehouse, consolidating tax-relevant data from multiple finance systems.
  • Corptax centralizes all tax data and allows automatic import from financial systems.

Process Automation and Workflow

All compared solutions support workflow management:

  • Oracle TRCS allows automation of routine tax adjustments and provides multi-user collaboration with Oracle EPM Task Manager for workflow.
  • ONESOURCE includes workflow tools and an Excel-based DataFlow interface for data collection.
  • Longview Tax has workflow capabilities through its portal for task assignment and status monitoring.
  • Vertex Enterprise focuses on automation of data integration and computations but may rely on external workflow tools.
  • Corptax features built-in workflow definitions to review data and enforce compliance steps.

Analytics and Reporting

Modern tax provision tools provide analytics to glean insights from tax data:

  • Oracle TRCS offers intuitive dashboards for real-time insight and integrates with Oracle Smart View for ad-hoc analysis.
  • ONESOURCE comes with interactive dashboards and allows drill-down from consolidated tax expense to entity-level details.
  • Longview Tax provides pre-built report templates and analytics to identify tax trends or variances.
  • Vertex Enterprise focuses on tax analysis across multiple tax types to discover strategic tax savings opportunities.
  • Corptax includes a reporting module for standard and custom reports and supports what-if scenario analysis.

2. Technical Architecture

The technical architecture impacts integration within an organization's IT landscape, as well as performance, scalability, and security.

Deployment Options

  • Oracle Tax Reporting is a 100% cloud-based SaaS solution on Oracle's cloud infrastructure with automatic updates.
  • Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE is offered as a cloud-enabled platform, with most modules accessible via web.
  • Longview Tax is now positioned as a cloud-based platform for corporate tax, supporting hybrid data connections.
  • Vertex Enterprise can be implemented on-premises or hosted, offering flexible deployment options.
  • CSC Corptax is available as a cloud/SaaS solution with secure hosting and web access.

Technology Stack and Performance

  • Oracle TRCS leverages Oracle's EPM platform with multi-dimensional database capabilities for fast consolidations.
  • ONESOURCE has a proprietary calculation engine optimized for tax computations with SQL-based databases.
  • Longview Tax performs real-time consolidation of tax data with an OLAP engine and relational storage.
  • Vertex Enterprise uses the Vertex Tax Performance Engine with a comprehensive tax database and standard connectivity.
  • Corptax is built on a centralized database model that efficiently handles enterprise-scale data.

Integration Capabilities

  • Oracle TRCS provides tight integration within Oracle's ecosystem, particularly with Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP and FCCS.
  • ONESOURCE offers ready-to-use APIs for ERP and consolidation systems, with certified SAP S/4HANA integration.
  • Longview Tax integrates with source systems through data connectors and Excel, automatically collecting data from financial systems.
  • Vertex Enterprise has pre-built integrations for major ERPs and can connect to source ledgers to transform data.
  • Corptax supports data import from ERP systems via standard formats, ODBC, or flat files.

Scalability

All solutions are built to scale to large enterprises:

  • Oracle cloud infrastructure scales computational resources as needed for complex calculations across hundreds of entities.
  • ONESOURCE is used by many large multinational corporations, demonstrating scalability for thousands of entities.
  • Longview Tax has been used by Fortune 500 companies for global tax reporting across many jurisdictions.
  • Vertex Enterprise was designed for complex, high-volume tax data with optimized data connectivity.
  • Corptax, in production at large corporates, scales to handle complex organizational data and multi-year calculations.

Security

All solutions implement enterprise-grade security including encryption, access controls, and regular audits.

3. Implementation and Support

Implementing a tax provision solution requires careful planning, data preparation, and partnership with vendors or consultants.

Implementation Approach & Timeline

  • Oracle TRCS offers relatively rapid implementations with out-of-the-box content and templates, often completed in a few months.
  • ONESOURCE implementations vary in length, with basic deployment taking a few months and full integration requiring more time.
  • Longview Tax implementations can be more involved due to flexibility but can be implemented in months with experienced partners.
  • Vertex Enterprise typically has the most complex implementation, often a multi-phase project spanning 6-12 months or more.
  • Corptax implementations are relatively straightforward, especially for U.S. tax provision, often under 3-6 months.

Customization Flexibility

  • Oracle TRCS works mostly via configuration with some custom calculations through business rules if needed.
  • ONESOURCE allows custom tax formulas, additional data fields, and Excel-based Workpapers for unique calculations.
  • Longview Tax offers high customization with options to design input forms and define calculation logic.
  • Vertex Enterprise is highly customizable in terms of data and process, often described as a "toolkit" for tax solutions.
  • Corptax leans more towards configuration than customization, with built-in logic for common tax differences.

Vendor Support and Training

All vendors provide support, training, and regular updates:

  • Oracle provides support through Oracle Support network and cloud customer connect community with monthly/quarterly updates.
  • Thomson Reuters offers phone/online helpdesk support, user training, and annual updates aligned with tax law changes.
  • Insightsoftware (Longview) provides customer support and a portal with account managers and technical support.
  • Vertex is known for strong customer support, timely tax updates, and extensive documentation.
  • CSC Corptax provides support through its team of former tax professionals and holds an annual user conference.

Partner Ecosystem

Each product has developed a network of partners:

  • Oracle has an extensive partner network of Big Four advisory firms and specialized Oracle implementers.
  • Thomson Reuters has long-standing relationships with tax consulting firms and technology partners for integration.
  • Longview partners include consulting firms specialized in performance management and tax.
  • Vertex partners with ERP vendors and global consultancies for implementation and integration.
  • Corptax is often implemented with help from tax advisors, with major firms like EY, PwC, and RSM having experience.

4. User Experience

User experience encompasses interface design, alignment with tax workflows, ease of administration, and overall user satisfaction.

Interface Design and Usability

  • Oracle TRCS uses a consistent web interface with Oracle's other EPM products, with forms, dashboards, and Excel integration.
  • ONESOURCE has been updating its interface, with a dashboard view and Excel-based DataFlow for familiar data entry.
  • Longview Tax provides a web portal with tight Excel integration, making it familiar for tax professionals.
  • Vertex Enterprise focuses more on back-end functionality with web dashboards and forms to review tax data.
  • Corptax has a modern web interface organized by tax process, with menu-driven data entry and Excel export capabilities.

Alignment with Tax Workflow

  • Oracle TRCS aligns closely with tax provision workflow, mirroring phases from data import to reporting with best-practice workflows.
  • ONESOURCE breaks the process into logical steps, with organized screens for data input, calculation, and reporting.
  • Longview Tax has highly customizable workflow that can be configured to match a company's specific process.
  • Vertex Enterprise focuses more on data flow than user task flow, ensuring proper sequence of data transformations.
  • Corptax aligns well with the corporate tax calendar, guiding users from trial balance import through final reporting.

Administrative Usability

  • Oracle TRCS is designed for functional users, with self-service administration requiring minimal IT involvement.
  • ONESOURCE provides a user-friendly admin panel for tasks like updating tax parameters and managing access.
  • Longview Tax administration can be more complex, requiring both tax knowledge and system expertise.
  • Vertex Enterprise administration is usually a specialized role, often requiring IT resources or dedicated tax technology support.
  • Corptax is generally easy to administer for knowledgeable tax professionals through its GUI.

User Satisfaction

User satisfaction varies based on functionality, ease of use, and support:

  • Oracle TRCS receives positive feedback from early adopters, especially for integration and time savings.
  • ONESOURCE is respected for reliability in tax calculations but has received criticism for UI and browser compatibility issues.
  • Longview Tax users praise its depth and real-time consolidation, though some note a learning curve.
  • Vertex users report high satisfaction with expertise and support, especially for those using multiple tax functions.
  • Corptax users are loyal due to comprehensive functionality and ease of use once trained.

5. Cost and Value

Cost considerations include licensing models, implementation costs, and the overall total cost of ownership (TCO) versus the value and ROI.

Licensing Models

  • Oracle TRCS uses subscription licensing (SaaS) based on number of users and environment size.
  • ONESOURCE typically uses a subscription model with tiers based on legal entities, complexity, or users.
  • Longview Tax follows an annual subscription model priced according to enterprise size.
  • Vertex Enterprise offers modular pricing for specific modules and tax content updates.
  • Corptax uses annual licensing based on entities and modules, often scalable for mid-size companies.

Implementation and Maintenance Costs

  • Oracle TRCS may have lower initial implementation cost due to faster deployment and cloud-based architecture.
  • ONESOURCE implementation costs vary based on consulting needs but include support and updates in annual fees.
  • Longview Tax may have higher implementation costs due to customization needs but offers strategic investment value.
  • Vertex Enterprise implementations represent a significant investment but provide a strategic platform for tax data management.
  • Corptax implementation is often handled by tax departments with limited vendor support, keeping initial costs lower.

Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

3-5 year TCO considerations include:

  • Oracle TRCS: Annual subscription plus initial setup, with continuous updates and potential bundled pricing with other Oracle products.
  • ONESOURCE: Annual fees with possibly separate support costs, with value increasing when using multiple modules.
  • Longview Tax: Significant license fees with support included, yielding efficiencies through integrated tax processes.
  • Vertex: Potentially highest initial cost but with value from centralizing processes that might otherwise require multiple solutions.
  • Corptax: Many companies find favorable TCO, especially when leveraging for both provision and compliance.

ROI Potential

All solutions aim to provide ROI through:

  • Accelerating the financial close
  • Improving accuracy and reducing errors
  • Enhancing visibility for tax planning

6. Situational Suitability

Solution suitability varies based on organization size, industry, geography, and technology landscape.

Organization Size

  • Large Multinational Enterprises: All solutions are viable, with ONESOURCE widely used for global compliance, Longview for complex consolidations, Vertex for unified tax data, Oracle for Oracle users, and Corptax for U.S.-focused companies.
  • Mid-size Companies: Oracle TRCS and Corptax often emerge as strong choices for mid-market companies, with ONESOURCE also serving this segment.
  • Small Companies: None are designed for small businesses, though Oracle (as part of an EPM bundle) or Corptax packages might be more accessible.

Industry Considerations

  • Financial Industries: Longview Tax has been popular due to strong consolidation capabilities.
  • Manufacturing, Retail, Consumer Goods: Vertex offers comprehensive tax solutions covering indirect and income taxes.
  • Technology and Pharma: ONESOURCE and Corptax handle detailed calculations for R&D credits and intellectual property.
  • Energy Sector: ONESOURCE or Corptax with customization for unique tax calculations.
  • Regulated Industries: All solutions provide audit trails, though ONESOURCE and Corptax have longer track records.

Geographic Complexity

  • Oracle TRCS supports multi-currency, multi-GAAP consolidation and BEPS CbCR reporting.
  • ONESOURCE is a global solution with localized versions and multi-currency reporting.
  • Longview Tax handles complex multi-jurisdictional data with support for multiple accounting standards.
  • Vertex includes global tax rules and caters to multi-jurisdiction needs.
  • Corptax has strong U.S. support with basic international consolidation capabilities.

Existing Technology Environments

  • Oracle ERP/EPM Users: Oracle TRCS provides seamless integration and consistent experience.
  • SAP Users: ONESOURCE and Vertex have advantages due to certified integrations and partnerships.
  • Longview/Insightsoftware Users: Longview Tax integrates well with existing Longview products.
  • ONESOURCE/Corptax Compliance Users: Staying within the ecosystem makes sense for data flow between provision and compliance.

7. Use Case Scenario Testing

Comparison of how each solution performs in specific real-world scenarios:

Use Case 1: Year-End Tax Provision Close (Consolidated)

Scenario: Company needs to aggregate trial balance data, calculate tax provision, analyze ETR, and produce tax footnote under tight deadline.

  • Oracle TRCS: Excels with Oracle financials integration, automated adjustments, and real-time consolidation. Built-in reports are generated directly for footnotes.
  • ONESOURCE: Uses DataFlow templates or API connections, with quick consolidated provision calculation and drill-down capabilities.
  • Longview Tax: Handles entity data entry with real-time consolidation, robust for late adjustments, and customizable tax footnotes.
  • Vertex: Pulls data into tax warehouse, applies tax rules, ensures granular entity-level accounting, and offers flexible analysis.
  • Corptax: Automates trial balance import, computes provision with automated U.S. state calculations, and produces footnote reports directly.

Use Case 2: Quarterly Provision and Forecast

Scenario: Tax department needs to compute estimated annual ETR, interim provision, and forecast cash taxes.

  • Oracle TRCS: Supports interim calculations with planning system integration for forecasted PBT and dashboards for comparison.
  • ONESOURCE: Handles interim provisions using estimated annual tax rates, with consistent rolling from interim to interim.
  • Longview Tax: Strong in combining actual provision and planning, enabling what-if analysis and scenario planning.
  • Vertex: Treats forecasting as another data scenario, with analytics for modeling changes and impact on liability.
  • Corptax: Allows provisional calculations with hypothetical data, though not primarily a forecasting tool.

Use Case 3: Global Tax Transparency and Reporting (CbCR, Pillar 2)

Scenario: Company needs to prepare country-by-country reports and prepare for Pillar 2 global minimum tax reporting.

  • Oracle TRCS: Includes out-of-the-box CbCR functionality with forms and dashboards, integrated into workflow.
  • ONESOURCE: Uses separate BEPS solution with data collection via DataFlow, leveraging existing entity data.
  • Longview Tax: Explicitly evolved for these needs, aligning provisioning with CbCR and Pillar Two in a single solution.
  • Vertex: Can store required metrics, requires configuration for Pillar 2, with users typically exporting for reporting.
  • Corptax: Offers International Compliance module for CbCR data and introduced Pillar Two functionality.

Use Case 4: Integration with Financial Close and Audit Support

Scenario: CFO wants tax provision integrated with financial close, and auditors require detailed workpapers and audit trail.

  • Oracle TRCS: Embedded in close timeline via Task Manager, provides read-only access for auditors with drill-down capability.
  • ONESOURCE: Offers drill-down from consolidated tax to entity adjustments, with Workpapers for documentation and controlled workflow.
  • Longview Tax: Provides audit trails, control with data validation, and customized workpapers/reports for audit committees.
  • Vertex: Maintains "as was" financial data, methodical adjustment handling, and detailed audit trail for evidence.
  • Corptax: Produces schedules supporting footnote numbers, integrates with document management, and logs all changes.

Scenario-Based Decision Framework

A suggested approach for evaluation:

  1. Identify Priority Criteria: e.g., "Fast close integration" or "Pillar 2 compliance" and assign weights.
  2. Score Each Solution: based on demos, references, and attributes discussed.
  3. Consider Scenario Alignment: Map solutions to your company's specific scenarios.
  4. Total and Compare Scores: Use weighted scores to narrow to top candidates.
  5. TCO and ROI Analysis: Develop a simple TCO calculator for finalists and compare to estimated savings.

Conclusion

In conclusion, each solution offers robust capabilities for corporate tax provisioning, but with different strengths:

  • Oracle EPM Tax Reporting is a modern, cloud-based solution ideal for organizations seeking tight integration with financial systems and rapid deployment of best-practice tax reporting, especially within Oracle-centric environments.
  • Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE Tax Provision is a tried-and-true leader, delivering comprehensive functionality from calculation to reporting to compliance in one platform, with solid integration capabilities.
  • Insightsoftware Longview Tax stands out for its holistic approach to tax management, aligning provision, planning, and emerging requirements like CbCR and Pillar 2 in a single system with deep customization.
  • Vertex Enterprise represents a comprehensive tax data management strategy for companies with high complexity or disparate systems, serving as a unified tax data hub for both direct and indirect taxes.
  • CSC Corptax provides a reliable, user-friendly environment for tax provision and beyond, especially favored by U.S.-based companies for integrated handling of federal, state, and international provisions and returns.

The choice depends on specific organizational needs and context. Companies should supplement this analysis with demonstrations, user references, and potentially pilot projects to ensure the selected solution not only scores well on paper but also aligns with workflows and delivers tangible improvements in the tax reporting process.
